MIT research found that responding to a lead within 5 minutes makes you 21 times more likely to qualify that lead compared to waiting 30 minutes. Auto AI Canada responds in 5 seconds.

## The Lead Decay Curve

Lead value drops precipitously over time:

- **0-5 minutes**: Peak conversion potential
- **5-30 minutes**: Significant drop in engagement
- **30+ minutes**: Lead likely contacted competitors
- **24+ hours**: Lead often already purchased elsewhere

## Why Humans Can't Compete

Even the best BDC team can't respond to every lead instantly. They're on calls, helping customers, at lunch, or simply overwhelmed during peak times.

## The AI Solution

AI agents respond the moment a lead comes in—whether that's 2 PM on Tuesday or 2 AM on Sunday. Every lead gets immediate, professional engagement.

## Quality at Speed

Fast doesn't mean robotic. AI responses are contextual, personalized, and conversational. Speed and quality aren't tradeoffs—they're both essential.

## Competitive Advantage

While your competitors' leads sit in queue, your AI is already building rapport. By the time a human BDC rep would respond, your AI has already qualified the lead and booked an appointment.

## Measuring Impact

Track your average response time before and after AI implementation. Most dealers see response times drop from hours to seconds—a metric that directly correlates with increased sales.
