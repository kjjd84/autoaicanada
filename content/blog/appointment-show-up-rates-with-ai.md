A booked appointment means nothing if the customer doesn't show up. Industry data suggests show-up rates hover around 50-60% for traditionally booked appointments. AI is changing that equation dramatically.

## The Confirmation Gap

Traditional BDC reps book appointments and move on. AI agents continue the conversation after booking, sending reminders, answering pre-visit questions, and building anticipation for the visit.

## Personalized Pre-Visit Engagement

Before the appointment, AI can:

- Confirm vehicle availability and prepare the unit
- Answer financing questions to reduce anxiety
- Provide directions and parking information
- Send day-of reminders with personalized details

## Reducing Buyer's Remorse

Many no-shows happen because buyers get cold feet. AI maintains engagement between booking and visit, addressing concerns and keeping excitement high.

## Real Dealer Results

"Our appointment show-up rate actually went UP after implementing AI," reports Marcus T., General Manager at a multi-location group. "The AI keeps customers engaged in a way our BDC never could."

## The Compound Effect

Higher show-up rates mean more opportunities for your floor staff. More ups lead to more sales. It's a virtuous cycle that starts with better appointment management.
