Across Canada, from the Maritimes to the West Coast, dealerships are discovering what AI can do for their bottom line. Here are stories from dealers who made the leap.

## Maritime Province Group

A three-store group in Atlantic Canada was struggling with after-hours lead response. Leads coming in after 6 PM sat until the next morning—often going to competitors who responded faster.

**Results after 90 days:**
- 45% increase in after-hours conversions
- Average response time dropped from 14 hours to 8 seconds
- Two additional sales per store per month from after-hours leads alone

## Ontario Metro Dealer

This high-volume store was drowning in leads. Their BDC couldn't keep up, and lead quality data was a mess.

**Results after 60 days:**
- 40% increase in overall vehicle sales
- CRM data quality improved dramatically
- BDC team redeployed to high-value activities

## Western Canada Family Dealer

A family-owned operation was skeptical about AI replacing the personal touch they'd built their reputation on.

**Results after 120 days:**
- AI handles 85% of initial conversations
- Owner personally handles VIP customers AI identifies
- 30% of old leads recovered and converted

## Common Themes

Every success story shares common elements: commitment to the process, willingness to let AI handle routine tasks, and focus on empowering (not replacing) the sales team.
