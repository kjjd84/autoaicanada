Sales pipeline management shouldn't require constant manual updates. Yet most dealerships still rely on reps to remember to change lead statuses—a task that often gets forgotten in the rush of daily operations.

## The Kanban Approach

Visual pipeline management using Kanban boards has transformed how dealers track leads. Columns represent stages: New, Engaged, Qualified, Appointment Set, Showed, Sold.

## The Manual Problem

When reps are busy with customers, pipeline updates suffer. Leads sit in wrong stages. Reports become unreliable. Managers can't trust their dashboards.

## AI-Powered Automation

Auto AI Canada automatically moves leads through your pipeline based on actual conversation outcomes:

- Lead responds → moves to "Engaged"
- AI qualifies interest and budget → moves to "Qualified"
- Appointment booked → moves to "Appointment Set"
- Customer confirms attendance → moves to "Showed"

## Real-Time Visibility

Your dashboard always reflects reality. No more end-of-day pipeline cleanup sessions. No more guessing which leads need attention.

## Customizable Stages

Configure pipeline stages to match your process. Add custom stages for your specific workflow. AI adapts to your dealership, not the other way around.

## Manager Benefits

Accurate pipeline data means better forecasting, smarter staffing decisions, and clearer accountability across your team.
