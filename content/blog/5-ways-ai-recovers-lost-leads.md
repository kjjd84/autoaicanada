Every dealership has them—leads that showed initial interest but went silent. Maybe they got busy, maybe they bought elsewhere, or maybe they just needed a nudge. Whatever the reason, these cold leads represent significant untapped revenue.

## 1. Automated Re-Engagement Campaigns

AI agents can systematically reach out to old leads with personalized messages based on their original inquiry. Unlike mass email blasts, AI conversations feel natural and responsive.

## 2. Inventory-Aware Outreach

When a lead originally inquired about a specific vehicle type, AI can reach back out when matching inventory arrives. "We just got a 2024 Silverado that matches what you were looking for" is far more compelling than a generic follow-up.

## 3. Timing Optimization

AI analyzes response patterns to contact leads at optimal times. Early morning, lunch breaks, and evenings often see higher engagement rates—and AI is always available.

## 4. Persistent but Polite Follow-Up

The AI follows up consistently without being pushy. It knows when to try again and when to back off, maintaining your dealership's reputation while maximizing recovery opportunities.

## 5. Automatic Pipeline Updates

As leads re-engage, the AI automatically updates their status in your CRM and moves them through your sales pipeline. No manual data entry, no leads falling through the cracks.

## The Numbers Don't Lie

Our dealers report recovering **30% of old leads** that were previously considered dead. That's revenue you already paid to acquire, now finally converting.
