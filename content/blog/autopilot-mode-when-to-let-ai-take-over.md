One of Auto AI Canada's most powerful features is Autopilot—the ability to let AI handle conversations automatically while maintaining the option to jump in at any moment.

## What Autopilot Does

When Autopilot is on, AI agents handle the entire conversation flow: greeting, qualifying, answering questions, and booking appointments. Your team monitors from the dashboard without needing to intervene.

## When to Use Autopilot

**Ideal scenarios:**

- After-hours lead response
- Old lead re-engagement campaigns
- High-volume periods (weekend traffic, sale events)
- Routine qualification and appointment setting

## When to Take Over

Sometimes human touch matters:

- Complex financing situations
- Upset customers requiring empathy
- High-value fleet or commercial inquiries
- VIP customers expecting personal attention

## Seamless Handoff

Switching between AI and human is instant. Take over a conversation mid-chat, handle the situation, then hand it back to AI. The customer experiences a seamless transition.

## Building Confidence

Start with Autopilot on for after-hours only. As you see the quality of AI responses, expand to more scenarios. Most dealers run Autopilot 80%+ of the time within 60 days.
