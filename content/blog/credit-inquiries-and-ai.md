Credit and financing questions are among the most sensitive in automotive sales. Dealers rightly wonder: can AI handle these conversations appropriately?

## The Skeptic's Concern

"I was skeptical," admits Sarah L., Internet Sales Director. "But watching the AI agent handle a complex credit inquiry in real-time sold me. It's faster and more polite than half my floor staff."

## How AI Handles Credit Topics

Modern AI agents are trained to:

- Gather preliminary information without making promises
- Explain general financing options without quoting specific rates
- Identify leads ready for finance manager handoff
- Maintain compliance with advertising and disclosure requirements

## Knowing When to Escalate

AI recognizes when conversations require human expertise:

- Specific rate quotes and payment calculations
- Complex credit situations
- Customer frustration or confusion
- Requests to speak with a manager

## Building Trust

AI handles initial credit questions professionally, setting up finance managers for success. By the time a human takes over, basic information is gathered and the customer feels heard.

## Compliance Considerations

Configure AI responses to match your compliance requirements. Set boundaries on what AI can and cannot discuss. Review conversation logs regularly to ensure quality.

## The Human Touch Still Matters

AI doesn't replace your finance team—it prepares customers for productive finance office conversations. The result: better prepared customers and more efficient F&I processes.
